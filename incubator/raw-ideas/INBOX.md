# Raw Idea Inbox

Use this file for low-friction capture of ideas that are too early for a standalone record.

## Rules

- Capture quickly.
- Preserve the user's wording when possible.
- Mark source and date.
- Do not validate during capture.
- Do not treat entries here as accepted memory.

## Entry format

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD — short title

- origin: user_raw_thought | model_captured_from_conversation | conversation_extract | repo_audit_finding | model_suggestion
- status: raw_idea
- core idea: ...
- context: ...
- next review trigger: ...
```
